The `landau_zener` benchmark is scored against the closed-form theory curve (ADR-0012, QHC-1944). The node reads the target's `simulated_points` table from its `landau_zener` experiment section in the calibration file (`phi_x`, `z_start`, `z_end` in Φ₀; `min_gap` Δ and `detuning_span` Δε in GHz) and sweeps each row's z ramp over the step's `ramp_durations`, which replace `flux_paths`. It reports the `rmse` similarity score S as the benchmark value, which the run scores against the tree's thresholds, now higher-is-better.

- One QProgram per (φx, T): the z ramp from `z_start` to `z_end` in T at the row's φx, between the entry and exit ramps. The measurement is one φx × T surface (`phi_x` outer, `ramp_duration` inner). A missing or malformed table, or fewer than four, non-increasing or fractional durations, fails the target before anything is measured.
- **`LandauZenerFit`** normalises the surface with the readout levels and compares it with `P_LZ = exp(−π²Δ²T/Δε)`: `S = 1 − D/D_ref` with D pooled over φx (trapezoid weights over φx and in log T), for the `rmse`, `rmse_excess` and `shape` variants. It also reports σ̂ and `fit_r2` from the decay fits, the gap ratio per φx and the uncertainty of S, and saves `score_s` and `rmse` (D) to the measurement entry.
- `NODE_REGISTRY["landau_zener"]` is now `higher_is_better`, so its thresholds need `passed` above `warning`.
