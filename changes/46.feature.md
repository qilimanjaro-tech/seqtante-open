The `t1` step runs as a `T1Node` (`FixedFluxNode`) benchmark at the sweet spot (QHC-1803).

- One T1 saturation sweep at the step's operating point, fit to an exponential decay. `analyze` reports the relaxation time and its uncertainty in microseconds; the run scores it against the tree's thresholds (higher-is-better). `apply` writes nothing, as a benchmark does (ADR-0002).
- The QProgram is the shared `t1_saturation` experiment from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1913), consumed as a dependency rather than copied: it reads the readout pulse and the drive/readout intermediate frequencies for the operating point from the calibration file. Seqtante-open keeps only the `t1_pulsed_saturation_experiment` driver, which sets the LOs and drive power on the platform, runs the sweep and streams the result. The exponential-decay fit (`T1Fit`) reports T1 ± its standard uncertainty and the fit R².
- `analyze` accepts a fit only when it is a physical decay (fitted rate `< 0`), its R² clears the step's minimum, and its relative uncertainty is within the step's maximum; otherwise the target fails the step. Every check runs before the fit is written, so a rejected fit never reaches the database.
- The fitted `t1` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). The `t1` benchmark reports in `us`.
