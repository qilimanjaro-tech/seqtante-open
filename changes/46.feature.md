The `t1` step runs as a `T1Node` (`FixedFluxNode`) benchmark at the sweet spot (QHC-1803).

- One T1 saturation sweep at the step's operating point, fit to an exponential decay. `analyze` reports the relaxation time and its uncertainty in microseconds; the run scores it against the tree's thresholds (higher-is-better). `apply` writes nothing, as a benchmark does (ADR-0002).
- The QProgram is the shared `t1_saturation` experiment from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1913), consumed as a dependency rather than copied: it reads the readout pulse and the drive/readout intermediate frequencies for the operating point from the calibration file. The node builds it and runs it directly -- no `experiment_class` driver layer -- then streams the sweep. The operating point's LOs, readout attenuation and drive source are set by `FixedFluxNode._set_operating_point_hardware`, shared by every fixed-flux node, through the restore handle: a Rohde & Schwarz source takes `drive_power` (dBm) with IQ modulation and RF on, a QCM-RF takes `drive_gain` ([-1, 1]) with hardware modulation on, and a readout attenuation that is not a whole number of dB is rejected. The exponential-decay fit (`T1Fit`) reports T1 ± its standard uncertainty and the fit R².
- Step keys are named like the `t1_saturation` arguments (`drive_ringup_time`, `drive_ringdown_time`, `drive_n_sigmas`, `readout_ringup_time`, `overlap_time`), with defaults in the node's `_DEFAULTS`. Idle durations are rounded, not truncated, so the stored time axis is the one played.
- `analyze` runs fit -> checks -> save -> plot. It accepts a fit only when it is a physical decay (fitted rate `< 0`), its R² clears the step's minimum (a flat trace's undefined R² is rejected rather than crashing the fit), and its relative uncertainty is within the step's maximum; otherwise the target fails the step and nothing reaches the database.
- The graph gives real nodes the same per-target `data_folder` (`<data_root>/<step>/<target>`) as legacy nodes, so their fits save plots there.
- The fitted `t1` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). The `t1` benchmark reports in `us`.
