Reconciled a benchmark's thresholds and direction onto one representation and moved benchmark scoring into the run.

- **One direction type and one thresholds type.** `BenchmarkDirection` (the `verdicts.py` enum) is now the single direction type, and `node_registry.Direction` (the `"higher"`/`"lower"` literal) is removed. The parser now produces `verdicts.BenchmarkThresholds` -- which gains a `unit` field -- as the single thresholds type, and the parser's own `Thresholds` is removed.
- **The run scores the benchmark.** A node's `analyze` reports the measured `value` and `uncertainty` on `AnalysisResult` (replacing its former pre-scored `benchmark` field); the run scores that value against the step's thresholds with `verdict_for` and builds the `BenchmarkResult`. A non-finite value, or thresholds whose unit or direction no longer match the benchmark, fails the target instead of being scored; a benchmark with no thresholds is logged and left unscored.
- Thresholds are carried on the step apart from its experiment parameters, so acceptance policy and experiment parameters no longer share one dict.
