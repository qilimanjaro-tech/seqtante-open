ADR-0012 settles the Landau-Zener open questions of ADR-0002: the theory curve is the closed-form two-level probability, computed from a per-qubit table of simulated points in the calibration file, and agreement is the similarity score S (three variants, the `rmse` one as the benchmark value), pooled over φx. It supersedes ADR-0003's χ²ᵣ metric and its lower-is-better direction for Landau-Zener.
