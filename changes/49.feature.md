The `t2` step runs as a `T2Node` (`FixedFluxNode`) benchmark at the sweet spot, consuming the shared `t2_ramsey` QProgram from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1809, QHC-1928).

- One T2 (Ramsey) DRAG sweep at the step's operating point, fit to a decaying oscillation. `analyze` reports the dephasing time and its uncertainty in microseconds; the run scores it against the tree's thresholds (higher-is-better). `apply` writes nothing, as a benchmark does (ADR-0002).
- The QProgram is built by `qili_qprograms.experiments.t2_ramsey` (itself the port of Qilitools' `t2__drag` / `t2_single`, QHC-1683): it plays the calibrated `pi_half-<operating_point>` pulse (or `pi-<operating_point>` at half amplitude) twice around an idle wait and reads the `readout-<operating_point>` pulse, its weights and the drive/readout IFs from the calibration file. The node builds it and runs it directly -- no `experiment_class` driver layer -- setting the operating point's LOs, readout attenuation and drive power/RF (and IQ modulation for a Rohde & Schwarz source) on the platform through the restore handle, then streaming the sweep. The decaying-oscillation fit (`T2Fit`) reports T2 ± its standard uncertainty and the fit R².
- The pi-half shape is now the stored `pi_half-<op>` (or half-amplitude `pi-<op>`) pulse rather than one rebuilt from `drive_duration`/`drive_amplitude`/`drive_sigmas`/`drag_coefficient` step parameters, and the idle sweep is unrolled inside one QProgram (qili's convention) rather than one QProgram per point. The local `qprogram/t2.py` and `experiment_classes/t2.py` are removed; the reusable measurement-id helper moves to `experiments/results.py`.
- The fitted `t2` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). The `t2` benchmark reports in `us`.
