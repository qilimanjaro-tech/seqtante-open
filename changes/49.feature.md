The `t2` step runs as a `T2Node` (`FixedFluxNode`) benchmark at the sweet spot (QHC-1809).

- One T2 (Ramsey) DRAG sweep at the step's operating point, fit to a decaying oscillation. `analyze` reports the dephasing time and its uncertainty in microseconds; the run scores it against the tree's thresholds (higher-is-better). `apply` writes nothing, as a benchmark does (ADR-0002).
- Ported from the Qilitools `t2__drag` experiment (QHC-1683): the `t2_drag_experiment` driver and the `t2_single` sequence (two DRAG pi-half pulses separated by the free-evolution wait, then a readout). The decaying-oscillation fit (`T2Fit`) reports T2 ± its standard uncertainty and the fit R².
- The fitted `t2` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). The `t2` benchmark reports in `us`.
