The `t2` step runs as a `T2Node` (`FixedFluxNode`) benchmark at the sweet spot, consuming the shared `t2_ramsey` QProgram from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1809, QHC-1928).

- One T2 (Ramsey) DRAG sweep at the step's operating point, fit to a decaying oscillation. `analyze` reports the dephasing time and its uncertainty in microseconds; the run scores it against the tree's thresholds (higher-is-better). `apply` writes nothing, as a benchmark does (ADR-0002).
- The QProgram is built by `qili_qprograms.experiments.t2_ramsey` (the port of Qilitools' `t2__drag` / `t2_single`, QHC-1683): it plays `pi-<operating_point>` at half amplitude twice around an idle wait, unrolling the idle sweep in one QProgram, and reads the `readout-<operating_point>` pulse, its weights and the drive/readout IFs from the calibration file. The node builds it and runs it directly, setting the operating point's LOs, readout attenuation and drive source through the base node's shared `_set_operating_point_hardware` (`drive_power` for a Rohde & Schwarz source, `drive_gain` for a QCM-RF), then streams the sweep. Optional step parameters default through the node's `defaults`.
- The calibration tree recalibrates only `pi-<operating_point>` (rabi), so a stored `pi_half-<operating_point>` pulse fails the target before any hardware is set: `t2_ramsey` would play it instead of half the freshly calibrated pi pulse.
- The fit (`T2Fit`) fits the trace both as a damped cosine and as a plain exponential decay and keeps the one with the lower BIC, so a Ramsey trace with no detuning is not fit to an oscillation seeded from noise. It reports T2 ± its standard uncertainty and the fit R².
- The fitted `t2` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). The `t2` benchmark reports in `us`.
- The node uses the helpers every node shares on `CalibrationNode` (QHC-1932, QHC-1936): it runs at the step's `operating_point` (`_operating_point_name`), and uses `_require_pulse` (a missing `readout-<operating_point>` fails the target before any hardware is set), `_sweep`, `_stream` (one acquisition per idle time), `_require_r2` and `_require_rel_uncertainty`. The idle window is `idle_duration_sweep`, as in `t1` and the qili-qprograms argument.
