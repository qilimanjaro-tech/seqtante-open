Added T1 (registered as `t1` in the calibration tree) energy-relaxation experiment along with its
fitter as an available fluxonium node.
It saturates the qubit with a smoothly ramped drive step, sweeps the idle time between the end of
that step and the readout, rotates the IQ plane so the decay collapses onto one quadrature and fits
`A * exp(B * t) + C` to it. `T1Fit` reports `T1 = -1 / B` and writes the trace, its fitted curve and
the fitted T1 to `<target>_T1.png` in the measurement's data folder.

The idle-time sweep is software-unrolled: the compiled qprogram carries one drive/readout block per
entry of `wait_sweep`, so the sweep length is bounded by the sequencer's program memory rather than
by a hardware loop.

Nothing is written back, because T1 is a benchmark rather than a calibrated parameter: no bus is
updated, and neither the runcard nor the `Calibration` is rewritten. The fit lives in the plot and
in the measurement stored in the database; recording it against the measurement itself waits on
`add_fitting` support in qililab's autocalibration DB.

Only qubit targets are measured; coupler tokens in `targets` are skipped, since a coupler has no
drive line of its own. A single T1 trace sweeps idle time alone, so there is no frequency sweep to
declare: both intermediate frequencies are read off the buses at run time (`Parameter.IF`), and the
drive LO is taken from the `Calibration`'s `LO` entry for the target when there is one, and from the
drive bus otherwise.
The `Calibration` at `calibration_path` must already carry a `CrosstalkMatrix`; the node raises
otherwise.
The parameters used are:
### **Required**

Written under the node's parameters; any of these can also be overridden per target via `overwrites:`.

- **wait_sweep** : `tuple[float, float, int]`.
  `np.linspace` args (start, stop, num) for the idle time between the end of the drive step and the
  start of the readout, in ns. This is the axis T1 is fitted against.

- **drive_amplitude** : `float`.
  Amplitude of the drive step, held as a DC offset for the whole of `drive_duration`.

- **drive_duration** : `int`.
  Length of the flat top of the drive step in ns, measured after the ring-up.

- **readout_amplitude** : `float`.
  Readout pulse amplitude.

- **readout_duration** : `int`.
  Readout pulse duration in ns.

- **averages** : `int`.
  Number of hardware averages.

- **relax_duration** : `int`.
  Relaxation wait between shots in ns.

### **Optional**

- **drive_gain** : `float`, default `1`.
  Output level of the drive source: power in dBm on a Rohde & Schwarz LO, gain on a QCM-RF.

- **overlap_time** : `int`, default `0`.
  Overlap between the end of the drive step and the start of the readout pulse (ns). It pulls the
  readout that much earlier. Capped to `min(readout_duration, drive_duration - 4)`, with a warning,
  rather than being allowed to overrun the drive.

- **drive_rise_time** : `int`, default `2_000`.
  Length of the smooth error-function ring-up played before the flat top, in ns. It precedes
  `drive_duration` rather than counting towards it.

- **n_sigmas** : `int`, default `4`.
  Gaussian sigmas spanned by that ring-up. Larger is a gentler ramp over the same duration.

- **q_relative_amplitude** : `float`, default `0`.
  Amplitude of the Q component of the ring-up, as a fraction of `drive_amplitude`, shaped as the
  derivative of the I component. Applies to the ramp only; the flat top is played on I alone.

## **Example**
```yml
t1:
  wait_sweep: [0, 40000, 81]
  drive_amplitude: 0.5
  drive_duration: 4000
  readout_amplitude: 0.075
  readout_duration: 2000
  averages: 1000
  relax_duration: 200000
  drive_gain: 0.8
  overlap_time: 2000
```
