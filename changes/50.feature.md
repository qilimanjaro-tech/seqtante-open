The `sweet_spot_flux` step runs as a `TwoToneVsFluxNode` (`FluxSweepNode`) that stores the measured sweet-spot flux (QHC-1804).

- The measurement is the shared `two_tone_vs_flux_saturation` experiment from `qili-qprograms` (SDK-403): a QDAC steps the flux of the step's `loop` (`z` or `x`) and, at every flux, the drive IF is swept with a saturation drive. The node builds it and runs it directly, as the `t1` node does; `uv.lock` moves `qili-qprograms` to `0.0.2.dev12+g9ab4450`.
- `flux_sweep` and `drive_if_sweep` are `np.linspace` arguments for offsets from the operating point's stored flux on the swept loop and its stored drive IF, so the same tree recentres after every recalibration. The stored map holds the absolute fluxes (Φ0) and IFs (Hz) played.
- The node places the operating point itself, right before the sweep: the unswept loop and the neighbours the point parks sit at their stored biases while the QDAC ramps the swept loop. The LOs, readout attenuation and drive source come from the shared `_set_operating_point_hardware`. The QDAC settle and pre-step times come from the QDAC low-pass filters unless the step sets both `flux_settle_duration` and `flux_pre_step_duration`; `repetitions` measures the map several times and stores the running average.
- `analyze` (`parabola_vertex`) fits a Lorentzian per flux and a parabola through the qubit IFs, and fails the target when there is no vertex in the window or the parabola's R² is below `min_r2` (0.8 by default) or undefined. `apply` writes the vertex into the operating point's flux on the swept loop (ADR-0011); the crosstalk matrix and its offsets are not touched.
- The fitted `sweet_spot_flux_z` / `sweet_spot_flux_x` (Φ0) and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791). They replace the unused `sweet_spot_flux_offset_z` / `_x` keys, since ADR-0011 stores the measured bias, not an offset correction.
- Coupler targets fail the step: the shared experiment drives and reads the target itself, and has no readout-qubit mapping yet.
- The legacy `two_tone_frequency_vs_flux_node` is no longer registered in `experiment_functions_dict`.
- `FittingClass` guards every fitted value (QHC-1932): it is generic in its results type, `fit()` assigns `results`, and reading `results` before a fit has assigned one raises `RuntimeError`. `T1Fit` (`T1Results`) and the flux model (`SweetSpotResults`) keep their outputs there, read through properties (`T1`, `r_squared`, `center`, …), so no method checks for a fit itself; `T1Fit.results` is now the mapping, with the `curve_fit` parameters under `params`. The flux model's `fitted` reports whether it found a sweet spot. Its parabola R² is NaN, not 0, when the fitted IFs have no spread, as for every other fit.
- Shared node helpers on `CalibrationNode` (QHC-1932), used by `t1` and `two_tone_vs_flux` here and by every other node as it lands:
  - `_sweep` / `_absolute_sweep`: a step's `np.linspace` window, optionally offset from an operating-point value, rejected under 2 points.
  - `_require_r2` / `_require_rel_uncertainty`: fail the target with `AnalysisError` against the step's `min_r2` / `max_rel_uncertainty`.
  - `_pulse` / `_require_pulse`: the `<purpose>-<operating_point>` pulse the shared QProgram plays (ADR-0008); a section naming another raises `ValueError`.
  - `_stream`: builds the `StreamArray`, runs the QProgram `repetitions` times and writes each execution with `write_per_point`, `write_whole` or the node's own (the flux map's running average). `experiments/results.py` goes.
  - `_section` and `_leaf_experiment`: a spine (no `experiment`) raises `TypeError` wherever a leaf is needed.
  - The step's `analysis` is passed to every node (`self.analysis`, the experiment's first by default).
- Step keys follow the qili-qprograms argument names: `t1`'s `idle_sweep` is `idle_duration_sweep` and `two_tone_vs_flux`'s `loop` is `flux_axis`.
- `FittingClass.save_plot` stores the plot's path on the measurement entry by default.
