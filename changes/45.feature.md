The `two_tone` step runs as a `TwoToneNode` (`FixedFluxNode`) at the sweet spot (QHC-1805).

- One two-tone pulsed-saturation sweep at the step's operating point, fit to a Lorentzian peak. The QProgram is `two_tone_saturation` from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1916), which takes the `readout-<op>` pulse, its weights and the readout IF from the calibration file; the node sets the drive/readout LOs, readout attenuation and drive power/RF through the restore handle. `readout_amplitude`/`readout_duration` are no longer step parameters, and `drive_ringdown_time` (default 200 ns) is new. `apply` writes the fitted drive IF onto the sweet-spot operating point and leaves the LO (placed outside the sweep) untouched. The legacy `save_platform` write is gone: the run persists the calibration file (ADR-0001).
- The fitted `drive_if`, `drive_lo` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791).
- `two_tone` now carries a `node_cls` in `NODE_REGISTRY` and is dropped from the legacy `experiment_functions_dict`. The Lorentzian fit is shared with `reset_point_search` (QHC-1807).
- `LorentzianPeakModel`'s plot-title prefix is configurable, so each caller labels its own sweep.
- A Rohde & Schwarz drive source now has its IQ modulation switched on before the sweep (in the `two_tone` node and each legacy `two_tone` driver), so it plays the IQ drive rather than a bare carrier.
