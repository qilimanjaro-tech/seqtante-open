A step runs at the `operating_point` it names in the calibration tree, and plays that point's `<purpose>-<point>` pulses, instead of always at its experiment section's point (QHC-1936). One target can now run the same experiment at two points: `rabi_reset` and `rabi_sweet_spot` measure at the reset and sweet-spot fluxes and write `pi-reset` and `pi-sweet_spot`. A step that names no point still runs at its section's.

- `CalibrationNode._operating_point_name(target)` and `_operating_point(target)` resolve the point. Placing the flux, `_set_operating_point_hardware`, `_pulse` and the point each node passes to its qili QProgram all use it, in t1 and two_tone_vs_flux. A step naming a point the target does not have fails with the target's points listed.
- A step's `pulses` is checked against the pulse the QProgram plays at its point. The section's `pulses` is checked too when the step runs at the section's point, and does not apply at any other point.
- The tree parser rejects, wherever they are set (step, `defaults` or override), an `operating_point` that is not a name and `pulses` that is not a mapping of known roles (`drive`, `readout`, `flux`, `fast_flux`) to pulse names.
- `operating_point_for`, `apply_operating_point` and `operating_point_flux_writes` take the point's name instead of the experiment: `(file, target, point)`.
