The `rabi` node consumes the shared `rabi_amplitude` QProgram from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1914) instead of a local copy.

- The QProgram is built by `qili_qprograms.experiments.rabi_amplitude`, which plays the calibrated `pi-<operating_point>` pulse (normalised to unit amplitude) through a gain sweep and reads the `readout-<operating_point>` pulse, its weights and the drive/readout IFs from the calibration file. The node builds it and runs it directly -- no `experiment_class` driver layer -- setting the operating point's LOs, readout attenuation and drive power/RF (and IQ modulation for a Rohde & Schwarz source) on the platform through the restore handle, then streaming the sweep.
- The drive shape is now the stored `pi-<op>` DRAG pulse rather than one rebuilt from `drive_duration`/`drive_sigmas`/`drive_drag` step parameters, and the gain average runs inside the hardware sweep (qili's convention). The local `qprogram/rabi.py` and `experiment_classes/rabi.py` are removed; the reusable measurement-id helper moves to `experiments/results.py`.
