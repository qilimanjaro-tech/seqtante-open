The `rabi` step runs as a `RabiNode` (`FixedFluxNode`): one Rabi amplitude sweep with two analyses, chosen per step (QHC-1806).

- The QProgram is the shared `rabi_amplitude` experiment from `qili-qprograms` (QHC-1914), consumed as a dependency rather than copied: it plays the calibrated `pi-<operating_point>` pulse, normalised to unit amplitude, through a gain sweep that averages inside the hardware loop, and reads the `readout-<operating_point>` pulse, its weights and the drive/readout IFs from the calibration file. The node builds it and runs it directly, as `t1` does. The operating point's LOs, readout attenuation and drive source come from the shared `_set_operating_point_hardware` (`drive_power` for a Rohde & Schwarz source, `drive_gain` for a QCM-RF).
- Step keys follow the qili-qprograms argument names: `drive_amplitude_sweep` (an `np.linspace` window), `averages`, `relax_duration` and `readout_ringup_time`; `min_r2` defaults to 0.9. The example tree's `rabi` defaults are updated to match.
- `readout_levels` writes the |0>/|1> readout levels, with their uncertainties, onto the operating point, tagged with the `readout-<operating_point>` pulse they were taken with. `pi_amplitude` (the default analysis) writes the fitted pi amplitude into the `pi-<operating_point>` DRAG pulse in `Calibration.waveforms`, keeping its other shape parameters. Both pulses must already exist (they come with the manual calibration): the node seeds none, and a missing one fails the step before any hardware is set.
- `RabiAmplitudeModel` fits the rotated signal to `FittingClass.cosfunc`, an undamped cosine of the drive amplitude, with an FFT frequency guess. The levels are its extrema, |0> being the one the first sweep point sits closest to, and the pi amplitude is the first point after it where the fit reaches the excited level. A flat trace has an undefined (NaN) R².
- `analyze` runs fit -> checks -> save -> plot through the shared `_require_r2`; a fit below the step's minimum R², or with no oscillation, fails the target and nothing reaches the database. The fitted `rabi_amplitude` and `fit_r2` are written to the measurement entry (QHC-1497, QHC-1791).
